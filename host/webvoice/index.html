Radio Voice Console

Speech at 250 bit/s over the OFDM modem. Hold transmit and talk; the receiving station writes what it decodes to a WAV file.

not connected

The link is warmed before every transfer. An idle station lets its rate ladder decay and falls back to listening on the slowest mode only, so a cold broadcast can go out at a rung the far end is not receiving on — which looks like a dead radio and is really a negotiation problem.

Speaker prompt
no prompt loaded — the far end will use the first 2 s of your own audio

The prompt decides whose voice comes out; the 250 bit/s stream carries only what was said. Load a binary prompt from roundtrip.py --prompt-out (float16 or int8, detected automatically) to decode as that speaker instead.

Decode profile
—

The far end decodes a second of speech at a time. It can wait for the next chunk before decoding this one, which costs latency and buys quality — measured against decoding the whole stream at once, one second of lookahead removes about 45 % of the chunking penalty and a second one removes 8 % more. Left context is free and always on; lookahead is the only part you pay for, so it is the only knob here. Changing it takes effect on the next transmission.
