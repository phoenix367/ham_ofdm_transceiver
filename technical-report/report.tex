\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This report documents a complete prototype of an audio-band OFDM
digital communication system for amateur HF radio, from a reproduction
of the PHY layer published by bashkirtsevich~\cite{habr_ofdm} to two
circuit boards exchanging files, packet radio, and speech over a wire.
The reproduction is bit-exact against every worked example in the
article (44/44 checks) and reaches $-7.2$\,dB sensitivity versus the
article's $\approx-7.5$\,dB on the same channel model; the residual is
diagnosed as synchronization loss, and three synchronization defects
--- a Zadoff--Chu time--frequency ambiguity worth $\sim$1\,dB, an
SNR-dependent tone metric, and a preamble transmitted 7\,dB below data
power --- are analysed and corrected.

The waveform is then extended with SNR-adaptive link modes that scale
the coherent symbol-tiling factor ($4\times/16\times/64\times$),
lowering the sensitivity floor to $-17.9$\,dB in the 6\,kHz
band\footnote{Throughout this report, SNR follows the article's
convention: total signal power versus total noise power in the full
6\,kHz Nyquist band of the 12\,kHz-sampled audio.  The signal itself
occupies only $\approx$2.1\,kHz (300--2400\,Hz), so the same operating
point reads $\approx$4.5\,dB higher against in-band noise only, and
$\approx$3.8\,dB higher in the ham-standard 2.5\,kHz convention
($-17.9$\,dB here $\approx$ $-14.1$\,dB ham).} (78\,\% of Shannon
capacity at $-20$\,dB); with a measured monotone LLR recalibration
worth 1.5--2\,dB at the sensitivity edge; with an IRA LDPC code,
Gray-mapped 16-QAM, and CRC-gated chase-combining HARQ; and with a
complete link layer: a 13-rung rate ladder spanning 7.8--1059\,bit/s
over $-17.9\ldots+4.7$\,dB, receiver-driven adaptation through a
20-bit piggybacked link-control word, QoS with fragment-boundary
preemption, selective-repeat bursts streamed behind one preamble, a
capability handshake, simplex channel access, closed-loop AFC netting
that pulls a $+284$\,Hz offset inside a 12\,Hz deadband in five
exchanges, and a non-acknowledged broadcast mode with a
sender-signalled statistics exchange that re-rungs under fading.

The model is followed by two implementations.  An integer-only
fixed-point twin of the receiver --- Q15 arithmetic,
block-floating-point FFT, NCO/CORDIC primitives, division-minimised
channel estimation --- matches the floating-point receiver within
0.5\,dB and surpasses it at the edge, and serves both as a golden
reference for a future RTL and as the arbiter for a bit-exact C99
implementation of the whole stack.  That implementation runs as
flash-resident firmware on two STM32H743 boards: a streaming receiver
whose memory follows the signal rather than the capture (10\,MB
$\to$ 561\,KB, flash 242 $\to$ 57\,KB), the EXTREME acquisition
brought from not keeping up to 9.5\,\% of the core by measurement on
the part, a DC-blind receive front end hardened by a stress campaign,
and a USB vendor-class modem protocol with a written specification.
On the two-board stand the link carries a 68\,kB file at 87\,\% of the
raw rung-12 rate with zero retransmissions, a 73\,kB file by broadcast
with none of 3089 frames lost, AX.25 through a KISS bridge and TCP
through a tunnel; an SDR puts the same stack on a 7\,MHz carrier and
decodes frames off the air.  Finally a 250\,bit/s neural speech codec
is evaluated and put on the air with push-to-talk between the boards
--- first audio 3.6\,s after the talker starts, decoded as it arrives
--- and the group loss it exposed on a clean wire is traced, after two
wrong explanations that survived their own measurements, to a receiver
commit defect that acknowledged modes had been hiding by
retransmission.

Every channel a figure was measured on --- the article's simulator, an
SSB RF chain with a physical oscillator model, a virtual-channel
daemon, the wire stand, the SDR path, and host harnesses that
reproduce board failures without boards --- is defined once, and every
result is regenerable from the repository's self-verifying suites
(44 article checks, 13 end-to-end, 28 fixed-point, 177 C, 91 host)
and recorded sessions any copy of the receiver decodes blind.
\end{abstract}
