\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{What Binds Performance Now}

\paragraph{The front end, not the code.}  The clearest lesson of the
coding work is that FEC choice stopped mattering before code quality
did: LDPC's 0.7\,dB AWGN advantage compresses to 0.3--0.4\,dB
end-to-end once the LLRs are calibrated, and to parity before, because
the demodulator's LLR quality is the binding constraint.  The monotone
recalibration recovered 1.5--2\,dB, but a standards-grade LDPC matrix
(802.11n/NR-style~\cite{ieee80211}) \emph{plus} fully calibrated LLRs
would be needed to realise the remaining $\sim$2\,dB.  Any further
sensitivity work should start at the LLR front end, not the decoder.

\paragraph{Synchronization is the sensitivity frontier --- and the
reliability frontier.}  The original reproduction gap, the fade-loss
analysis, and every whole-group loss measured on the boards point the
same way: frames are lost to detection and timing, not to decoding.
HARQ helps only in the noise-limited regime for exactly this reason ---
a sync-level loss leaves no LLRs to combine.  The two-board stand made
the point at the \emph{other} end of the SNR range, twice: the
two-lag CFO unwrap recovered $\sim$8\,\% of all acquisitions that ARQ
had been silently repairing, and the voice campaign found the
streaming detector committing a weak spike two blocks early on a wire
with 25\,dB of margin.  Both defects were in the acquisition path,
both were invisible to every acknowledged mode, and both were found by
a mode that cannot retransmit.  A link layer that repairs losses is
also a link layer that hides them; broadcast is the instrument that
measures the receiver.

\paragraph{Physics caps EXTREME.}  The $64\times$ mode's 0.69\,s
coherent integration assumes a channel coherent over that window
($\lesssim$0.1\,Hz Doppler spread).  This is a physical ceiling, not
an implementation one, and it is why the mode ladder --- rather than
ever-longer integration --- is the right architecture.

\paragraph{Feedback latency at the floor.}  Once a direction falls to
EXTREME, the adaptation loop period is bounded by 25--45\,s frame air
times.  The controller is designed around this (fade-aware filtering,
loss fallback, silence decay), but no tuning can make a 7.8\,bit/s
control loop fast.  The same arithmetic governs broadcast: a
statistics block is 45\,s and a reply window 9.4\,s because the
exchange has to fit inside a plausible fade, and a stream to a peer
whose ladder has decayed is held while probes bring it up rather than
sent into a deaf receiver.

\paragraph{On the boards, the binding constraints were time constants
and ownership, not DSP.}  The demodulator passed the whole
input-abuse matrix bare (Section~\ref{sec:cport_harden}); every field
failure of the stress campaign lived in carrier sense, in a timer
shorter than the receiver's 2.3-s end-of-frame commit, in a DAC parked
off mid-rail, or in a piece of state that two owners disagreed about
(Section~\ref{sec:cport_review}).  The recurring shape was a policy
correct for the traffic it was written for --- full groups only, a
sentinel packed into a wire field, a probe that no code path cleared
--- becoming a defect when a new class of traffic arrived.  The fixes
that lasted made the policy a choice a caller can decline rather than
changing it.

\paragraph{Measurement on the part corrected the projections.}  Every
processor figure quoted before the boards existed was a projection on
an assumed 400\,MMAC/s; the part delivers 147, and the EXTREME
acquisition that was projected at 10\,\% of the core measured 27\,\%
and, in its first flash-resident form, did not keep up at all.  The
figures that matter --- 9.5\,\% of the core, 561\,KB, 2283\,ms worst
blocking commit --- are all measured, and two of the three
architectural decisions they forced (re-anchoring the ZC scan, sizing
the capture FIFO by the worst burst rather than the average) went the
opposite way from the projection.

\subsection{Limitations}

The sensitivities are measured on a simulation.  The channel model is
faithful to the article and extended with Rayleigh fading and a
physical oscillator model, but the wire stand cannot fade or
attenuate, and the SDR measurements are of the radio, not of a
propagation path: the off-air decode used an AM transmitter, and no
frame of this waveform has yet crossed an ionospheric path.  The
measured sensitivities should be planned around with $\sim$3\,dB of
fading margin ($-14\ldots-15$\,dB for EXTREME), and the link budget's
receiver-noise figure belongs to a wideband unpreselected SDR.

The 16-QAM rungs run without LLR recalibration (the map is
BPSK-trained; a modulation-specific map is straightforward but
unmeasured).  The LDPC matrix is a from-scratch IRA construction, not a
standards matrix.  Streamed bursts, broadcast and the C stack are
conv-FEC only.  The Python station does not mirror the C stack's
capability handshake, pooled storage or block statistics --- it is the
reference for the ladder and ARQ, not for the extensions.

The voice evaluation is bounded the same way as everything else: the
codec's catastrophic-failure rate was measured on read speech one
corpus away from training, the Russian results are one sentence and
one narration, and the enrolment exchange is designed but not yet on
the air.  The USB product ID is pid.codes' shared test identifier, and
Windows has no binding.

\subsection{Future Work}

In rough order of expected value: the same frames over a real HF path,
which the boards, the SDR driver and the recorded WAVs are all ready
for; a standards-grade LDPC matrix plus QAM-specific LLR
recalibration; porting the fixed receiver's slew-limited tracker back
to the float model (it is measurably better); pilot reduction or 2-D
channel estimation to reclaim the 30\,\% of the grid the pilots
occupy; the voice mode's enrolment exchange on the air and a
fine-tuned vocoder, whose language-identification confidence gives the
fine-tune a ready objective (\S\ref{sec:voice_status}); a second
board-side channel that can fade --- an attenuator and a fading
simulator in the wire --- so the block-statistics laws proven on the
harness are proven on converters too; per-carrier bit-loading on the
measured channel estimate; a 46.875\,Hz-spacing variant for very
stable channels; and shrinking EXTREME's frequency-search range once
AFC netting has converged, which is compute on the part.
